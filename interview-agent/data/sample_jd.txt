Senior Backend Engineer — Distributed Systems

About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Platform Engineering team.
You will own the design, implementation, and reliability of our core microservices
infrastructure serving 50 million daily active users.

Requirements (Required)
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong Python proficiency (3.10+), including async patterns and type hints
- Deep knowledge of distributed systems concepts: consensus, CAP theorem, eventual consistency
- REST API design and OpenAPI specification authoring
- SQL databases: query optimisation, schema design, indexing strategies (PostgreSQL preferred)
- Experience designing and operating microservices in production
- Strong understanding of observability: structured logging, metrics, distributed tracing
- Ability to mentor junior and mid-level engineers

Requirements (Preferred)
- Kubernetes orchestration and Helm chart authoring
- Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka or equivalent)
- Familiarity with gRPC and Protocol Buffers
- Cloud infrastructure on GCP or AWS
- Contributions to open-source projects

Key Responsibilities
- Design and own multiple backend microservices end-to-end
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Drive reliability improvements: SLO/SLA definition, incident response, post-mortems
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct code reviews
- Collaborate with product, data, and ML teams to deliver platform features

Compensation
Competitive salary, equity, and comprehensive benefits.
